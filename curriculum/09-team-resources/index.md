# Team Resources

Each Flow Research team maintains a curated page of resources — links to articles, tools, repositories, and relevant curriculum lessons — to help fellows ramp up and go deep in their area.

## Teams

- [Decentralized Training](01-decentralized-training.md) — distributed training infrastructure and federated learning
- [Reinforcement Learning](02-reinforcement-learning.md) — RL research, environments, and applications
- [Local-First Computing](03-local-first-computing.md) — offline-first, sync, and edge architectures
- [Workstream / Jarvis](04-workstream-jarvis.md) — task pipelines and the collaboration protocol
- [Harnessy](05-harnessy.md) — agent reliability, evaluation, and testing
- [Security](06-security.md) — protocol and application security
- [Learning Content](07-learning-content.md) — curriculum design and educational materials
- [Creative Media](08-creative-media.md) — design, video, and technical communication
- [Economics](09-economics.md) — tokenomics, incentives, and economic modeling

## For Team Leads

Each team page is a markdown file under `curriculum/09-team-resources/`. Team leads own the `Key Resources` and `Tools & Repositories` sections on their pages. To add or update team-owned guides, tools, repositories, datasets, workspaces, or operational references, edit your team's file and open a pull request. See the [contributing guide](../08-contributing/index.md) for details.
