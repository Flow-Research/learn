# Economics

The Economics team designs incentive structures, tokenomics, and economic models for the Flow Research ecosystem. Work spans mechanism design, game theory modeling, reward distribution systems, and the economic analysis of agent-human labor markets.

## Getting Started

- [Mechanism Design Theory in Control Engineering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.00756) — by **Ioannis Vasileios Chremos and Andreas A. Malikopoulos**. Tutorial introduction to mechanism design for engineering systems.
- [Designing a Token Economy: Incentives, Governance, and Tokenomics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09608) — by **Samela Kivilo et al.** End-to-end design guidance for token economies.
