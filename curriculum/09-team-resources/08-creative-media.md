# Creative Media

The Creative Media team produces visual, video, and written content to communicate Flow Research's work. This includes technical illustrations, explainer videos, diagrams, presentation decks, and social media assets that make complex ideas accessible.

## Getting Started

- [Mermaid](https://mermaid.js.org/) — diagramming and charting syntax used throughout the curriculum.
- [Excalidraw Documentation](https://docs.excalidraw.com/) — public documentation for sketching architecture diagrams and visual explanations.
