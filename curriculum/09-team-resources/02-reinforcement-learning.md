# Reinforcement Learning

The Reinforcement Learning team applies RL to agent behavior, task optimization, and autonomous decision-making. Work spans from classic RL algorithms to modern approaches like RLHF and offline RL.

## Getting Started

- [Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html) — by **Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto**. The canonical textbook on RL.
- [Spinning Up in Deep RL](https://spinningup.openai.com/) — by **OpenAI**. A hands-on educational resource for learning deep reinforcement learning.
- [Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.06339) — by **Yuxi Li**. A survey of deep RL algorithms and techniques.
