# Decentralized Training

Decentralized training brings together distributed compute resources to train machine learning models without relying on a single centralized cluster. The team explores federated learning, gossip-based gradient sharing, and coordination protocols for heterogeneous nodes.

## Getting Started

- [Federated Learning: The Grand Tour](https://federated-learning.org/) — by **Peter Kairouz et al.** Overview of federated learning concepts, challenges, and open problems.
- [Distributed Training of Deep Learning Models: A Taxonomic Perspective](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.03970) — by **Matthias Langer et al.** A survey of data and model parallelism strategies.
